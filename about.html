About

Hello, I’m John.

I’m an in-house graphic designer at Laborde Products, a marine engine distributor in Covington, Louisiana, and a Bachelor of Design student at Loyola University New Orleans.

I started at Laborde as a graphic design intern in May 2026 and moved in-house that August, designing print ads, product guides, internal systems and social content. At Loyola I work across print, motion and interactive type.

“Design is about making connections: connecting ideas from clients to their community. It should be easy to understand, but leave people with something to think about.”
How I work

Open about AI.

I see AI as part of a designer’s future, and I’m happy to use it when it helps the work. I’m also clear about where it’s used: the case studies note where AI was and wasn’t used. My poster, print and type projects are designed entirely by me, with no AI. Where AI helped, like the code behind input + output, the project says so.
