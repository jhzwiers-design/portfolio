About

Hello, I’m John.

I’m an in-house graphic designer at Laborde Products (a marine engine distributor in Covington, Louisiana) and a Bachelor of Design student at Loyola University New Orleans.

I started at Laborde as a graphic design intern in May 2026 and moved in-house that August, designing print ads, product guides, internal systems and social content. At Loyola I work across print, motion and interactive type.

“I think design is about making connections. It’s about taking ideas and finding ways to communicate them to the people they’re meant for. Good design should be easy to understand, but still leave people with something to think about.”
How I work

Open about AI.

I see AI as part of a designer’s future, and I’m happy to use it when it helps the work. I’m also clear about where it’s used: the case studies note where AI was and wasn’t used. My posters, books and type projects are designed entirely by me, with no AI. Where AI helped, like the hero images in two of my Laborde ads or the code behind input + output, the project says so.
