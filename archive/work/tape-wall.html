The brief

A group wall made of tape, built around two design mentors and a quote.

Yokoo uses hard lines to create both a pattern and direction or movement in many of his works, and given the medium, those elements would be communicated most effectively in tape. Both designers use a heavy amount of design elements, layering over each other into very dense compositions.

Using the quote, we could show direction through a forced perspective effect behind the text. Given that the two halves of the quote are of similar size, we could use them for symmetry, or to show asymmetrical movement between the two halves.

Define the problem

Our mentors were Tadanori Yokoo and Aries Moross, and our quote was “Don’t aspire. Achieve.” by Amanda Gorman. We chose three principles from their work: repetition and pattern, movement and direction, and layering.
