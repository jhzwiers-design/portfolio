The brief

Make an exactly 30-second trailer for a film found on Archive.org, branded for a real TV network, with type in the network’s own typeface and a logo animation at the end.

I pitched three colorized science fiction films. Earth vs. the Flying Saucers was the one I found most interesting, and a film about aliens fit best on SYFY.

Class
Motion Design 2, Loyola
Professor
Matt Normand
When
Jan 26 – Feb 11, 2026
Tools
After Effects, Premiere Pro, Illustrator
AI use
None. I edited, set the type and animated it myself in Premiere Pro and After Effects.

From the pitch to the final cut.
