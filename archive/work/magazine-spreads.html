The brief

Lay out an existing magazine article across three spreads: a cover spread and two spreads of text and images.

The article, Nature Does It Better by Zach Mortice, is about biomimicry: designing buildings and products that imitate the models, systems and elements of nature, from adhesives modeled on gecko feet to turbine blades that mimic whale fins.

Outcome

A cover spread, then two of the article.

The final cover sets the title over a curved, ribbed building facade. The two spreads that follow run the article in columns, with photographs of the examples it describes: gecko feet, a whale-fin turbine blade, the Shell Palace and the Supertree Grove.

The headline is set in Miller Banner, the subheads in ITC Avant Garde and the body text in Baskerville.
