The brief

The evolution of my packaging began with early sketches that were geometric and focused on the sustainable values of NICHE. From the start, I wanted to make packaging that could be collected, which meant creating a variety of designs and layouts, especially for the hang tags.

An important idea carried through the lineup was ‘making’ and customization. This is expressed through the slogan system that developed during refinement, where each product has a call to action beginning with “MAKE IT YOUR” and ending with a word that matches the product. These customized taglines create unity across the set while still giving each item individuality.

For production, all paper tags are made from off white cardstock, chosen for both durability and its natural, sustainable look. The resin 3D printed stamps are reused across the packaging, reinforcing consistency and sustainability. Design Smart appears in the use of layered colors, where only two inks can be combined to create multiple palettes and effects.
