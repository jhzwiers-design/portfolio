The work

Strawberry Shortcake.

The final spread, October 23.

The bowl of strawberries on the left, the recipe on the right.

The brief

Set a recipe for strawberry shortcake as a magazine spread: the title, an introduction, the ingredients and the preparation steps, with a photograph.

The recipe comes from Jane Grigson’s Fruit Book, adapted by Nancy Harmon Jenkins. The spread went through three rounds of versions, twenty-five in all, before the final on October 23.
