The work

Back, spine and front.

The final cover, April 2: 13 × 9 inches, green on the front and orange on the back.

Back, spine and front.

Green for the vibrant Earth people long for, with the title and author in Adso, set into black openings in the grid.

Orange for the dry, desert-like world of the story. The synopsis comes from the book’s own back cover.

Circles hidden among the squares.

Made after the class as personal work. The cover was never printed.

The brief

Redesign the full cover of a novel: front, spine and back. There were four books to choose from, and we were not supposed to look at the existing covers so they would not influence our designs.

I chose Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? because I wanted to design a cover for a science fiction book. The book is about androids blending in with people, so the circles blend in with the squares: organic circles against mechanical, geometric squares.

The colour

Two landscapes.

The colour comes from the two main settings in the story. Orange is the desert wasteland. Green is the pasture and the planet of life.

I wanted the viewer to see the lively green first, then turn to the back and find the orange wasteland, in the same order these landscapes come up in the story.
