The work

Three concepts.

Concept 1, shapes. Because the Tabasco sauce is associated with being full of flavour, I wanted to animate explosions to symbolise the burst of flavour from the sauce. For inspiration I looked at the scene in Disney’s Ratatouille where flavours appear as fireworks.

Concept 2, peppers. Because of the freshness of Tabasco hot sauce, I wanted to incorporate photography of fresh peppers in my design.

Concept 3, pour. Because the Tabasco logo is most associated with the sauce, I wanted the animation to look like the sauce was being applied.

The brief

Explore an existing logo in motion, using animation as a metaphor for the brand. Everyone in the class animated Tabasco.

Three animated logos, each 3 to 5 seconds, on black. The logo lands in the center at about a fifth of the screen width and holds for a second before fading to black. Sound was required, and the process went into a printed process book. Partway through, we cut the concept we liked least to focus on the best two.
