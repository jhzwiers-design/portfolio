The brief

Design a 12-page type specimen book for News Gothic: 7 × 7 inches, black and one additional colour, with headlines, text settings and complete character sets.

I began by researching the history and designer of the typeface, then made mood boards and sketched layouts. Through that research I found that News Gothic was the typeface on the first Scrabble tiles, and that became the theme of the book.

1Feb 13–18

A typeface from 1908

Morris Fuller Benton designed News Gothic in 1908 for American Type Founders. It became a go-to for newspapers and magazines, and it was on the first American Scrabble tiles.
