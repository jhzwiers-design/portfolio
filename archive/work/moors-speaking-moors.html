The brief

Conversations become words. Words descend into the film.

The audience never touches anything, uses a camera or speaks into a microphone on purpose. They just talk while they wait, and their conversation becomes part of the projection.

How it works

Listen, recognise, choose, respond.

A microphone listens to the room. Speech recognition picks out the words people say, and words from a list connected to the play drop into the film.

1 · ListenA microphoneHears the room. Nobody speaks into it on purpose.
→
2 · RecogniseSpeech recognitionPicks out the words people say.
→
3 · ChooseWords from the playA list connected to the play sets which words enter and how they move: whisper, drift, reveal, column, monolith or echo.
→
4 · RespondInto the filmThe loudness of the room sets how many words appear, how big and how much they move.

When the room is quiet, a standby screen invites people to “Say something.”

The words

Forth, freedom, voice.

The vocabulary file sets which words can enter the film and how they move: whisper, drift, reveal, column, monolith or echo. The loudness of the room sets how many words appear, how big and how much they move.
