The brief

For this project, I’ll be creating different animations for the individual letters D, E, S, I, G, and N. The final outcome will be a collection of animated GIFs that will be used on the Design Department’s website.

Once all the animations are done, The Collective will combine everyone’s letters to spell out the word DESIGN. All of my letters will be shown on a plain white background. The process will mix both analog and digital methods; I’ll begin with hand-drawn sketches before moving into digital animation.

I’ll be generating motion adjectives that connect to graphic design, typography, art, UX/UI, and even onomatopoeia. In total, I’ll be responsible for creating 100 variations of letters.
