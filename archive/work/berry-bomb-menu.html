The brief

Project 1 of Typography Lab 2: design a menu for a smoothie bar. Up to three colours, with photography allowed, but it is a type assignment: photos, patterns and colour should never overpower the readability of the type.

The goals were line, letter and paragraph spacing for readability, grids, basic InDesign, and using scale, contrast and balance to build hierarchy, with words and prices that communicate value. It moved from sketches on paper into InDesign over five rounds of review.

Outcome

Pink for the sections, green for the rest.

The final menu sets four sections of five smoothies each, Berry Classy, Protein Power, Healthy in Green and Fun & Fruity, then a list of add-ons and boosters.

Section titles are pink, item names and prices green, all in Montserrat, with the prices aligned to the right of each column.
