The brief

Design a typeface: Z87 began as a revival of a monospaced grotesque, grew a Cyrillic alphabet, and ended on posters.

The posters set Z87 over historical colour photographs of the Russian Empire: Dinner During Haymaking, Children’s Group and the Chusovaya River, each in English and in Russian.
