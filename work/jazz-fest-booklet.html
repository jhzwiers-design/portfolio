The brief

A printed schedule booklet for the 2025 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival, from the client’s text file of every band, stage and time over two weekends.

Black plus two colors, no bleeds, 11 × 17 inch sheets folded on the long side. The cover is a treble clef: one side sits in the blue background as negative space, and the gold shape it leaves behind reads as a close-up of a horn.

1April 3

Research

I collected jazz festival posters and schedules on one board and wrote down the words for the feel I wanted: negative space, minimal, structural contrast, bold shape. Then I sketched covers built from music notation.

Jazz festival posters, schedules and Swiss design.

Clefs, staff lines and piano keys.

Five covers

3Typefaces

Three typefaces

I set the same schedule spread in CoFo Sans, Neue Haas Grotesk and Degular and printed them to compare. CoFo was a branch out from the typeface you would expect in a Swiss-style booklet.

Setting the schedule.

4April 12

Blue and gold

Some of the festival happens in the morning and some at night. Through critique I chose gold and a darker blue to stand for the day and the night. I also tried a spring green, and set it aside.

Cover options, April 12.

Iterating until I found the version I liked.

An option I explored and set aside.

5Versions 1–4

Inside the covers

Version 1 was printed April 10 with no designed inside cover. Version 2 put music notes on the inside, version 3 swapped them for the bass clef, and version 4 removed the notes from the whole booklet.

Notes on the inside: the outside had a clef, so I put music notes inside.

The notes became too basic, so I used the bass clef on the inside covers.

Music-note accents beside the week.

The notes removed.

6Navigation

A week on every spread

Adding a page for each week would have made it 16 pages and raised the cost of printing at scale. Instead every spread is marked, set in Prisma, a typeface made of lines like the lines of a staff.

Set in Prisma at the foot of the page.

Someone halfway through a week never has to turn the page to find out which week it is.

Week in blue, then the day, the stage in blue italic, and each time in bold gold beside the band in black. Times are set with a GREP style.

7April 22

Printing

I printed color tests until the blue was rich and moody and the gold looked golden, then printed the booklet and put it together with a book stapler.

Long and narrow: 5.5 × 17 inch pages.

The covers fanned out.

Printed and stapled by hand.

The schedule inside.

Outcome

A clef in the background, a horn in the foreground.

Gold and blue for the day and the night, every spread marked by week, and CoFo Sans as a less expected choice for a Swiss-style booklet. No AI was used: the cover was drawn in Illustrator and the layout set in InDesign.

That’s the process.
