The brief

Laborde Products distributes Mitsubishi marine engines across North America. The Power Guide needed a redesign: updated and expanded to show the engine series, compare them with competitor engines, and share stories from vessels that repowered.

I designed it in InDesign as facing-page spreads, inside Laborde’s navy and orange brand.

Client
Laborde Products
Role
Design and layout, in-house
When
Summer 2026
Tools
InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator
AI use
None. Laid out in InDesign, with the cover made in Photoshop.

Outcome

Commercially printed, and online.

The guide was printed by an industrial printer and came back as a finished piece. I also built a web-only version with live links to Laborde’s social media and maps.
