The brief

Laborde distributes Mitsubishi, Scania and Steyr Motors marine engines. Over the summer they needed print ads across those brands: product ads, boat announcements, and a new remote monitoring product.

These weren’t updates to existing work. I designed each ad from start to finish, handling the layout, text and photo compositing, and built reusable templates so the team could make future ads themselves.

AI use
Two hero images started with AI: the Steyr unmanned vessel, made before official imagery existed, and the LYNC phone on a pedestal. Both were refined and composited by hand in Photoshop. Layouts and copy are my own, and the new construction template used no AI.
Outcome

In print, and easier for the next person.

Over the summer I produced a new ad about every other week. Ten ran in WorkBoat and Waterways Journal. The two congratulations templates and the rest of the assets went onto Laborde’s SharePoint so the team can keep using them. The lesson I took away: some of the most valuable design work is the system that helps other people work faster.

Steyr Motors

Before official imagery existed.

I had to show an unmanned surface vessel before any official photos were released, so I generated the vessel with AI from reference images, then refined it, designed the ad and wrote the copy in Photoshop. Twelve versions to one headline: Maximum power. Minimum mass. Mission ready.

Congratulations templates

Thirteen concepts, two templates.

Laborde congratulates customers when a vessel is repowered or newly built. I turned thirteen concepts into two templates the team fills in themselves. For new vessels, I based the design on the christening: the champagne bottle breaking on the bow. No AI was used.
