The brief

Design a 12-page type specimen book for News Gothic: 7 × 7 inches, black and one additional colour, with headlines, text settings and complete character sets.

Through research I found that News Gothic was the typeface on the first Scrabble tiles. That became the theme of the book.

Process

How it came together.

The process in seven steps, one image from each. Hover or tap an image for the note.

7 steps · drag or use the arrows

Morris Fuller Benton designed News Gothic in 1908 for American Type Founders.

A mood board and a sketch for each theme: sci-fi, a split-flap display, newspapers and magazines, and Scrabble.

The title set on tiles and laid out like words on a board.

The book took the name Type Tiles, with each letter’s Scrabble point value, and every character on its own tile in four styles.

A radial design built only from different characters of News Gothic.

Refinements across the whole book, printed as a mockup.

The italic sets moved to tan type on black, and the large “gothic” gave way to the radial pattern on a full tan page.

Outcome

A deep dive into book design.

This project was a deep dive into book design, layout systems, and the details that bring a typeface to life on the page.
