The brief

Create a brand and its packaging in nine rounds: research existing packaging and brainstorm names, take a logo from sketch to final, then apply it to the front and back of a package.

Northbrew is a tea company I invented, brewed in the Tongass National Forest in Alaska and packed in a resealable, waterproof pouch for hiking. Brewed by nature. Built for the trail.
