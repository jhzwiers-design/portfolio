The brief

The Bloom Festival is held in remembrance of Kendall Daigle. Each student took one section of her book, A Soul Under Construction, and made a projection for the chapel using type only: the section title leads, and the poem titles come second.

My section was LOVE, with seven poems: Act of Love, Star of David, Ann Noel, Pack of Wolves, Perfection, The Last Night Ever and Love at Last.

In the final, the titles of the poems rotate in 3D space around the central idea of love. Having ribbons of text around it almost felt like a ribcage around the heart of love.

Type from seven countries

Research before any type moved: typography from the United States, Holland, Japan, Mexico, Russia, Germany and Georgia, with a note on what each one taught me.

Experimental Dutch type that treats letters as shapes, and how type interacts with the space on the page.

Designs that extend the geometric nature of Japanese script and make type the main element.

Repetition and geometric sans-serifs that follow strict grids and internal logic.

A very unique script, in historical and modern interpretations.

Four concepts

Four advanced storyboards, each with a main scene and four screenshots, and each built as a moving test in After Effects.

LOVE pulses in the center like a heartbeat, its letters connected like the poems. The titles rotate around it, holding it together.

The camera moves around LOVE as it breaks into simple shapes, while the titles revolve around the blocks: organic and geometric forms side by side.

Warping emotions: a simple distortion that ends up as a sphere of text absorbed into itself, held together by the titles.

The poem titles warp and move over LOVE, and interact as they cross.

5Review 5

One concept, combined

I chose the sum of shapes. When I moved the 3D camera through it, the compositions looked like large blocks with dynamic type on them, which reminded me of historical type posters from the Netherlands. It also had an idea that feels a little complex, not basic. Professor Normand liked the concept, but wanted me to combine the ideas from the drafts and have the words wrap around a video in the middle of the type. That is what the final does.

Close-up

Type that wraps in a ribbon.

I used Tangle, a tool from DIA, to set the poem titles in a ribbon. Then I made LOVE in the middle in After Effects, brought the clips together and layered them with blend modes, and used the 3D camera to make the space feel three-dimensional.

Reflection

More than one kind of software.

What I’m most proud of is how dynamic the type feels, and that I used more than Adobe software and explored other web tools. Being a designer is about using all the tools, learning new software and being able to adapt.

To push the project further, I would expand it to the other chapters of the book in similar animation styles. Other students in the class made those chapters, and together they did not feel completely cohesive, so that is what I would want to improve.
