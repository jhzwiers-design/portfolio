The brief

Laborde distributes Mitsubishi, Scania and Steyr Motors marine engines. Over the summer they needed print ads across those brands: product ads, boat announcements, and a new remote monitoring product.

These weren’t updates to existing work. I designed each ad from start to finish, handling the layout, text and photo compositing, and built reusable templates so the team could make future ads themselves.

AI use
Two hero images started with AI: the Steyr unmanned vessel, made before official imagery existed, and the LYNC phone on a pedestal. Both were refined and composited by hand in Photoshop. Layouts and copy are my own, and the new construction template used no AI.

I had to create a visual for an unmanned surface vessel before official imagery had been released. I used AI through multiple generations, giving it reference images of similar vessel types to develop a vessel that fit the concept. Then I brought the result into Photoshop to refine the image, add the final design and write the copy.

It was a fun challenge that pushed me to experiment with new tools while taking creative ownership of the final design.

Congratulations ads

Thirteen concepts, two templates.

Laborde congratulates customers in print when a vessel is repowered or newly built. I explored thirteen concepts, then turned the strongest into two templates the team can fill in: one for repowers and one for new construction.

Repower: 13 concepts → template

The challenge was a full-page congratulatory ad for Waterways Journal that could be reused for different vessel repowers, while staying simple enough for other team members to update in Photoshop. The style evolved over many iterations as I explored layouts, visual treatments and ways to balance flexibility with strong branding.

The final template makes the vessel the focal point, keeps clearly organized, editable layers for easy updates, and uses Laborde’s brand pattern to create depth between the foreground and background, so future edits are fast and consistent.

New construction: the smashed bottle

A companion to the repower template, for when a new vessel is built with Laborde’s engines. The two ads needed to feel like one campaign while celebrating different milestones. I researched boat christenings and watched videos of the ceremony. Its highlight is breaking a bottle of champagne on the bow, so that moment of celebration is what I wanted the ad to capture: the bottle smashing, and the boat emerging from it.

It also had to be simple enough to use as a template. I made a base image, composited it in Photoshop, and used layers to isolate the boat and mask it into the champagne from the bottle. No AI was used. After many iterations on the composition, it became a clean, reusable template that stands out among the more cluttered ads in Waterways Journal.

Outcome

In print, and easier for the next person.

Over the summer I produced a new ad about every other week. Ten ran in WorkBoat and Waterways Journal. The two congratulations templates and the rest of the assets went onto Laborde’s SharePoint so the team can keep using them. The lesson I took away: some of the most valuable design work is the system that helps other people work faster.
