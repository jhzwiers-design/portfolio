The brief

The Bloom Festival is held in remembrance of Kendall Daigle. Each student took one section of her book, A Soul Under Construction, and made a projection for the chapel using type only: the section title leads, and the poem titles come second.

My section was LOVE, with seven poems: Act of Love, Star of David, Ann Noel, Pack of Wolves, Perfection, The Last Night Ever and Love at Last.

In the final, the titles of the poems rotate in 3D space around the central idea of love. Having ribbons of text around it almost felt like a ribcage around the heart of love.

Reflection

More than one kind of software.

What I’m most proud of is how dynamic the type feels, and that I used more than Adobe software and explored other web tools. Being a designer is about using all the tools, learning new software and being able to adapt.

To push the project further, I would expand it to the other chapters of the book in similar animation styles. Other students in the class made those chapters, and together they did not feel completely cohesive, so that is what I would want to improve.
