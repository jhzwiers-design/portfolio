The work

Brewed by nature. Built for the trail.

Rooibos Red Tea in a resealable, waterproof pouch, with the Northbrew mark on a misty forest.

Rooibos Red Tea over a misty temperate rainforest.

The company story, then ingredients, nutrition facts and the barcode.

The pouch with loose tea.

The brief

Create a brand and its packaging in nine rounds: research existing packaging and brainstorm names, take a logo from sketch to final, then apply it to the front and back of a package.

Northbrew is a tea company I invented, brewed in the Tongass National Forest in Alaska and packed in a resealable, waterproof pouch for hiking. Brewed by nature. Built for the trail.

Class
Print Design and Narrative, Loyola
Professor
Lisa McKinley
When
April 6 – 30, 2025
Tools
Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop
AI use
None. The name, logo and package are my own design, made in Illustrator, InDesign and Photoshop.

1April 6–7

Research

I photographed existing teas from every side to see how each panel does its job: the name and image on the front, the story and ingredients on the back, the brand on the sides and top.

Three rooibos teas: top, left, front, right and back.

Laid side by side, the panels show what each one carries.

2April 9

Thirty-six sketches

Logo sketches for three names: Northbrew, Steep North and Steep & Stir, each tried with arrows, mountains, mugs, tea bags and leaves.

Three names, many marks.

Arrows, mountains, mugs, tea bags and leaves.

Thirty-six sketches in all.

3Round 3

Ten refined

Northbrew was the name that resonated with my peers and with Professor McKinley. Her signature at the bottom of the sheet approved the direction.

Ten refined sketches.

4April 14

Into Illustrator

Fifteen black and white versions. The arrow stands for navigation and direction, tea that can be taken on the go. The mountain is a line that climbs.

In colour

6Round 6

The mark

The mountain line sits over the wordmark, with TEA CO tucked under the last letters.

The mountain line stands for the upward direction and the climb.

7April 22

Mood board

The colour came from this board and the packaging references I found and liked. Then thumbnails for placing the logo on the front.

They resonated with the outdoor feel of the brand.

Thumbnails trying the logo in banners, tags and badges.

8April 28

Four fronts

A pattern of leaves, a single tree, a graphic mountain range and a misty forest, each with the logo in a badge. The misty forest resonated with the company story I wrote and with the Alaskan forest the tea comes from.

A single tree.

A graphic mountain range.

The direction I chose.
