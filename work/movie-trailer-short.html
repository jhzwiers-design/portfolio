The brief

Make an exactly 30-second trailer for a film found on Archive.org, branded for a real TV network, with type in the network’s own typeface and a logo animation at the end.

I pitched three colorized science fiction films. Earth vs. the Flying Saucers was the one I found most interesting, and a film about aliens fit best on SYFY.

Process

How it came together.

The process in six steps, one image from each. Hover or tap an image for the note.

6 images · drag or use the arrows

For the first review I pitched three colorized science fiction films, each with three networks that could air it.

Spaceships that form the Y, a flying saucer that beams the logo in, and bubbling science flasks.

I cut the first edit, set single words over the footage, and ended on the SYFY logo.

I wrote the copy and set it in SYFY Hero, the network’s typeface, with the key words in yellow-green.

I showed the cut with the explosions, including the scene where I mapped the words onto the building.

Ten scenes in 30 seconds, ending on the logo inside the film’s own footage.

Outcome

Ten scenes in thirty seconds.

Two full cuts came in at 32 and 33 seconds with black-and-white explosion shots. The final drops them and comes in at 30 seconds.
