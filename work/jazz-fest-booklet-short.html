The brief

A printed schedule booklet for the 2025 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival, from the client’s text file of every band, stage and time over two weekends.

With over a hundred performances across 12 stages, the goal was to make the schedule easier for fairgoers to navigate: clear type hierarchy, consistent formatting and a strong grid, still connected to the spirit of Jazz Fest. The limits were strict: black plus two colors, no bleeds, 11 × 17 inch sheets folded on the long side, and only the typefaces we had used that semester. The cover is a treble clef: one side sits in the blue background as negative space, and the gold shape it leaves behind reads as a close-up of a horn.

Outcome

A clef in the background, a horn in the foreground.

Gold and blue for the day and the night, every spread marked by week, and CoFo Sans as a less expected choice for a Swiss-style booklet. It was printed, folded and stapled as a complete prototype. The project pushed me to work efficiently with a large volume of text and sharpened my layout and booklet design. No AI was used: the cover was drawn in Illustrator and the layout set in InDesign.
