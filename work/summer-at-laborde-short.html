The brief

Laborde Products is a marine engine distributor in Covington, Louisiana, with locations in Paducah, Kentucky, and Houston, Texas. As their graphic design intern, every day was different: print ads, product guides, internal documents, apparel, social media and a mural.

I came in wanting three things: to put my studio training to work on real deliverables that get printed, published and used; to design for an audience outside the design world; and to grow as a professional, not just as a designer.

The print ads and the Mitsubishi Marine Power Guide have their own pages. This page is the rest of the summer.

Reflection

Design for the people who come after you.

Some of the most valuable design work isn’t the most noticeable. It’s the systems and templates that help other people work faster: the congratulations ad templates, the training system, and a SharePoint archive I reorganized so the team won’t hit broken links later.

I came in split between in-house and agency work, and found I love in-house design: one familiar client to focus everything on. I’m returning to Loyola with a stronger portfolio and a better sense of how design works outside the university.
